Continuous motion generation

Unlike video concatenative libraries that snap between pre-recorded clips, the diffusion denoiser models continuous joint trajectories, producing natural transitions and coarticulation between signs.

Grammatical non-manual markers

Facial expressions, eyebrow movements, and head orientation are synthesized jointly with hand gestures to distinguish question intent, negation, and grammatical emphasis in full signed sentences.

Sub-400ms streaming latency

DDIM sampling and an incremental WebSocket generator stream animation chunks directly to the web avatar as each sign is synthesized, enabling live conversational turn-taking without offline rendering delays.
