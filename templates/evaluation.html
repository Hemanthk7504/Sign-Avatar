Quantitative Realism Metrics

Calculates algorithmic benchmarks comparing diffusion motion generation against human motion capture.

  • Average Position Error (APE): Spatial error in shoulder-width units (lower is better).
  • Dynamic Time Warping (DTW): Temporal alignment invariant to signing speed (lower is better).
  • Jerk Ratio: Third derivative of motion relative to real signers (1.0 = matches human capture).
  • Diversity: Pairwise sample distance across different signs (detects mode collapse).
